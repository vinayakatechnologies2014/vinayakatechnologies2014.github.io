Privacy Policy

Buzz ("Buzz", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information that may be collected when you visit buzz.peoplesblog.co.in.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect information such as:

Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you:

  • Contact us by email
  • Submit feedback
  • Send a correction request
  • Submit information about a story
  • Subscribe to any available newsletter or notification service
  • Participate in forms or other features on the website

This information may include your name, email address, message, and any other information you choose to provide.

Please do not send passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information unless specifically required for a legitimate purpose.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on our hosting, analytics, advertising, security, and other service providers.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Approximate location derived from technical information
  • Website usage and interaction information

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Investigate correction requests
  • Improve website content and functionality
  • Understand website usage
  • Detect security issues and abuse
  • Prevent fraudulent or malicious activity
  • Maintain website performance
  • Analyze audience trends
  • Display advertising, where applicable
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the website without an appropriate legal basis or permission where required.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Buzz may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, analytics, security, personalization, and advertising.

Third-party services used on the website may also place cookies or similar technologies on your device according to their own policies.

You can control certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website.

Analytics information may include pages viewed, traffic sources, device information, approximate geographic information, and other usage statistics.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

Buzz may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to their applicable policies and your available choices.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party advertising providers.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, services, or other external resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of those third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect our legitimate interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

9. Children's Privacy

Buzz is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights relating to access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or other forms of control over your personal information.

Requests may be submitted to:

Email: contact@peoplesblog.co.in

We may need to verify the identity of the requester before processing certain requests.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests:

Email: contact@peoplesblog.co.in

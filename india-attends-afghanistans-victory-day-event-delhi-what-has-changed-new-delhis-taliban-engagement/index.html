India Attends Afghanistan’s 'Victory Day' Event in Delhi: What Has Changed in New Delhi’s Taliban Engagement?

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Tue, 18 Aug 2026 - 10:34
Verified

India participated in a diplomatic event in New Delhi marking the fifth anniversary of the Taliban's return to power in Afghanistan.

The event was held at the Afghan Embassy in New Delhi on August 17, 2026. M. Anand Prakash, Joint Secretary handling the Pakistan-Afghanistan-Iran division in India's Ministry of External Affairs, attended the reception as chief guest.

The event marked August 15, 2021, when the Taliban entered Kabul and returned to power following the withdrawal of US-led international forces from Afghanistan.

India has not formally recognised the Taliban government as the government of Afghanistan.

The attendance of an Indian senior foreign ministry official at the event is nevertheless a confirmed development in India's diplomatic engagement with the authorities in Kabul.

What happened in New Delhi?

The Afghan Embassy in New Delhi hosted a reception on August 17 to mark the fifth anniversary of what the Taliban calls "Victory Day."

Afghanistan's Chargé d'Affaires in New Delhi, Mufti Noor Ahmad Noor, addressed the gathering.

Diplomats and representatives from several countries also attended the reception.

The event was significant because it publicly brought together representatives of the Taliban-run Afghan diplomatic mission and an Indian senior foreign ministry official.

India's diplomatic engagement with Afghanistan

India's diplomatic presence in Afghanistan changed after the Taliban took control of Kabul in August 2021.

Indian diplomatic personnel were evacuated from Afghanistan following the Taliban takeover.

In June 2022, India returned a technical team to Kabul to coordinate humanitarian assistance and maintain diplomatic communication.

India subsequently increased its engagement with Kabul.

In October 2025, Afghan Foreign Minister Amir Khan Muttaqi visited India.

The visit was followed by further diplomatic and economic contacts between India and Afghanistan.

India also upgraded its diplomatic presence in Kabul.

These developments occurred before the August 2026 Victory Day reception.

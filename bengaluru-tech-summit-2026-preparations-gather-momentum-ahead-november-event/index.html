Bengaluru Tech Summit 2026 Preparations Gather Momentum Ahead of November Event

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Fri, 24 Jul 2026 - 18:06
Developing

The 29th Bengaluru Tech Summit (BTS 2026) is scheduled to take place in November 2026, with the event preparing to bring together technology companies, startups, investors, researchers, policymakers and other industry participants. The official summit website describes the 2026 edition as the next chapter of the event's technology-focused programme.

The central focus of this year's summit is AI and emerging technologies, with the official programme covering areas including artificial intelligence, DeepTech, quantum technologies, advanced computing, robotics, aerospace and advanced manufacturing. The breadth of the programme shows how the summit has expanded beyond conventional IT and software discussions.

The BTS 2026 conference is planned as a three-day programme, with multiple thematic tracks and sessions bringing together technology leaders, startup founders, researchers, investors and policymakers. The official website says the programme will include keynote sessions, fireside chats, panel discussions, masterclasses and roundtable discussions.

One of the major areas of attention will be DeepTech and frontier technology. The announced tracks include AI, next-generation computing, quantum technologies, robotics, aerospace, advanced manufacturing and advanced materials, reflecting the growing importance of technology that goes beyond traditional software development.

The summit will also cover technology applications across several industries. The official programme includes tracks focused on health and biotechnology, electric mobility, digital infrastructure, data centres, cybersecurity, assistive technology, financial technology and education technology.

Another significant component is the India-USA Tech Conclave, which is designed to bring policymakers, technology leaders and research representatives together to discuss emerging technologies, regulatory issues, investment opportunities and international collaboration.

Startups are also expected to have a prominent role in the event through programmes such as the Future Makers Conclave, startup pitching, investor connections, mentoring and innovation-focused activities. These programmes are intended to connect emerging companies with investors, industry participants and potential partners.

The summit's programme also reflects Bengaluru's growing position as a centre for AI, DeepTech and technology-driven innovation. Rather than focusing on a single technology sector, BTS 2026 is bringing together multiple emerging fields and examining how they could influence businesses, industries and society.

For people following the event, the coming months are likely to bring more information about speakers, detailed sessions, participating companies, startup programmes and other activities. Those details can evolve as the summit approaches, which makes this a story that should continue to be updated rather than treated as a completed event report.

The Bengaluru Tech Summit 2026 is therefore still a developing story. The event itself and its broad technology focus have been officially announced, while additional programme and participation details can be added as they are confirmed by the organisers.

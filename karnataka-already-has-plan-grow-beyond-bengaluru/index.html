Karnataka Already Has a Plan to Grow Beyond Bengaluru

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Thu, 3 Sep 2026 - 09:13
Verified

The idea of developing a second Bengaluru is not starting from zero.

Karnataka already has government programmes aimed at expanding technology, innovation and economic activity beyond the state capital.

The Department of Electronics, Information Technology and Biotechnology and the Karnataka Digital Economy Mission have been working on the Beyond Bengaluru initiative.

The objective is to encourage economic and technology growth in cities outside Bengaluru and create opportunities for companies, startups and skilled professionals across the state.

Six Beyond Bengaluru Technology Clusters

The Government of Karnataka's Local Economy Accelerator Program (LEAP) identifies six Beyond Bengaluru technology clusters.

They are:

  • Mysuru–Chamarajanagar
  • Mangaluru–Udupi
  • Hubballi–Belagavi–Dharwad
  • Tumakuru
  • Kalaburagi
  • Shivamogga

These clusters are being developed through different programmes and interventions aimed at strengthening innovation, entrepreneurship and technology activity outside Bengaluru.

Investment in Regional Innovation

The Karnataka Government has approved an outlay of ₹1,000 crore over five years for the Local Economy Accelerator Program, with ₹200 crore allocated for the current financial year.

The programme includes initiatives such as incubators, accelerators, innovation labs and startup support, along with other measures intended to strengthen emerging technology centres.

The government has also established innovation and startup infrastructure in several cities outside Bengaluru.

The Difference Between a Second Bengaluru and Beyond Bengaluru

The two ideas should not be treated as exactly the same.

The Beyond Bengaluru programme focuses on developing multiple cities and regions across Karnataka.

The new suggestion from NITI Aayog is to seriously consider developing another major urban centre that could help reduce the pressure on Bengaluru.

The Karnataka Government has not yet announced whether any particular city will become the proposed second Bengaluru.

For now, Karnataka already has a broader strategy of encouraging growth across multiple cities outside Bengaluru.

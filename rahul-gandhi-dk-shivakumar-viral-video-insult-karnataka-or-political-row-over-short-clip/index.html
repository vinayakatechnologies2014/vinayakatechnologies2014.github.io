Rahul Gandhi – DK Shivakumar Viral Video: Insult to Karnataka or Political Row Over a Short Clip?

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Fri, 16 Oct 2026 - 12:43
Verified

A viral video featuring Rahul Gandhi and Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar during an INDIA bloc protest march in New Delhi on October 12 has triggered a political controversy. The BJP accused Rahul Gandhi of disrespecting Karnataka’s Chief Minister, raising the question of whether the incident was an insult to Karnataka or a political row built around a short video clip.

The video shows Rahul Gandhi moving through a crowded march, with Shivakumar appearing to be moved back amid the crowd. BJP leaders criticised Rahul, arguing that the interaction was disrespectful to Karnataka’s elected leader.

Shivakumar, however, rejected the claim that Rahul had pushed or humiliated him. He explained that Rahul was concerned about the large crowd and had suggested that he move towards a vehicle for safety. Deputy Chief Minister G. Parameshwara also pointed to crowd movement and security arrangements as possible reasons for the physical jostling.

The controversy has prompted debate about Karnataka’s dignity, political loyalty and the use of viral videos in political battles. Some critics view the footage as disrespectful, while Rahul’s supporters point to Shivakumar’s own explanation that the incident was misunderstood.

However, social media reactions and statements from political leaders cannot establish what the majority of Kannadigas think. There is no reliable statewide public-opinion data confirming that Karnataka as a whole considers the incident an insult.

A key question remains: should the interaction be judged by a few seconds of video, or should the wider context and Shivakumar’s explanation also be considered? A direct public response from Rahul Gandhi would provide further clarity, but a deliberate insult cannot be established from the clip alone.

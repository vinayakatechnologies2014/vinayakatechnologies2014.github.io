Karnataka to Prepare Plan for a Possible Second Bengaluru: What Happens Next?

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Thu, 3 Sep 2026 - 10:14
Developing

Karnataka is set to prepare a comprehensive plan after NITI Aayog suggested that the state should consider developing a second Bengaluru.

The suggestion was made by NITI Aayog Vice Chairman Dr. Ashok Kumar Lahiri during a meeting with Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar in Bengaluru on September 2, 2026.

Lahiri said developing another major urban centre could help reduce the pressure on Bengaluru.

Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar said Karnataka would prepare a plan based on NITI Aayog's suggestions and study initiatives taken by other states to develop new growth centres.

No City Has Been Selected

At this stage, the government has not officially selected a city to become Karnataka's second Bengaluru.

There is also no announced project for building a completely new city under that name.

The immediate step is to prepare a plan and examine possible approaches.

Karnataka already has the Beyond Bengaluru programme, which focuses on developing technology and economic activity in several cities and regions outside Bengaluru.

What Could Happen Next

The next stage will depend on the plan prepared by the Karnataka Government.

Possible areas of consideration could include:

  • Infrastructure
  • Employment
  • Technology and startups
  • Industrial development
  • Transport connectivity
  • Investment
  • Housing
  • Education and skilled workforce

However, these are areas that could be considered in a future urban-growth plan. They should not be treated as announced features of a "second Bengaluru" at this stage.

The Current Status

The proposal is currently at an early planning stage.

NITI Aayog has made the suggestion, and the Karnataka Government has said it will prepare a comprehensive plan.

Until further decisions are announced, there is no official confirmation of which city, if any, will be developed as Karnataka's second Bengaluru.

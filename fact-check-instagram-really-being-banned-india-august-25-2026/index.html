Fact Check: Is Instagram Really Being Banned in India From August 25, 2026?

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Sat, 22 Aug 2026 - 09:41
Fact Checked

A message circulating on social media claims that Instagram will be banned across India from the night of August 25, 2026.

The claim is false.

The PIB Fact Check Unit, the Government of India's official fact-checking mechanism, has rejected the claim and said that no such announcement has been made by the Government of India.

What Is the Claim?

A viral message says that Instagram will stop operating in India from August 25, 2026 because of a government decision.

The message is being shared as an official-looking update, leading some users to believe that Instagram is about to be blocked nationwide.

What Did the Government Say?

The PIB Fact Check Unit has clarified that the claim is fake.

There is no Government of India announcement confirming an Instagram ban from August 25, 2026.

The government fact-checking unit has specifically addressed the viral claim rather than leaving it unverified.

Is There an Official Ban Order?

No.

There is no government notification or official announcement confirming that Instagram will be banned across India on August 25.

The PIB Fact Check portal is the official Government of India platform for submitting and checking claims related to the Central Government.

PIB Fact Check – Government of India

What About India's Social Media Rules?

India does have rules governing social-media platforms and digital intermediaries.

Those rules can require platforms to follow various legal and regulatory obligations. The government can also take action against specific unlawful content or online services when permitted under applicable law.

However, these regulations do not mean that Instagram is being banned on August 25, 2026.

There is currently no official government communication establishing such a nationwide ban.

What Should Users Do?

Instagram users do not need to delete their accounts or take any action because of this message.

If you receive the claim, don't forward it as an official announcement.

Instead, check:

  • Official Government of India websites
  • PIB Fact Check
  • Government notifications or press releases

A social-media post or screenshot claiming to be a government announcement should not be treated as genuine without verification.

The Verdict

Claim: Instagram will be banned in India from August 25, 2026.

Fact: The Government of India's PIB Fact Check Unit has rejected the claim and confirmed that no such announcement has been made.

Verdict: FALSE

As of August 22, 2026, there is no official Government of India announcement confirming an Instagram ban from August 25.

Don't share the rumour as fact. Check the official source first.

We value feedback from our readers, sources, organizations, businesses, and members of the public.

If you have a question about an article, want to report a factual error, wish to provide additional information, or have a concern about content published on Buzz, please contact us.

For Corrections

If you believe an article contains an incorrect or outdated factual statement, please provide:

  • The URL of the article
  • The specific statement you believe is incorrect
  • The correct information
  • Supporting evidence or a reliable source, if available

We will review legitimate correction requests and may update the article when appropriate.

Please understand that submitting a correction request does not guarantee that an article will be changed. Editorial decisions are made after reviewing the available information and evidence.

For Source Information

If you have information about a developing story, please clearly distinguish between:

  • Information you personally know
  • Information you have received from another person
  • Information available publicly
  • Information that has already been reported elsewhere
  • Information that is speculation or unconfirmed

Providing supporting documents, public records, or other verifiable evidence can help us evaluate information more effectively.

Content Removal Requests

If you believe content published on Buzz violates your legal rights, privacy rights, copyright, or other applicable rights, please contact us with the relevant details and supporting information.

We will review legitimate requests in accordance with applicable law and our editorial policies.

Important

Buzz does not guarantee publication of information submitted by readers or members of the public.

We may independently evaluate information before publishing it, and information submitted to us may not necessarily be published.

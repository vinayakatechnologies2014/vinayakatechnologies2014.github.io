India Launches BIO-NIVESH to Push Private Investment Into Biotechnology

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Fri, 4 Sep 2026 - 10:40
Verified

India has launched BIO-NIVESH, a new platform intended to connect biotechnology innovators with private investors and increase access to capital for technologies approaching scale-up and commercialisation.

Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh launched the initiative on September 3.

The first edition brought together 20 biotechnology and deep-tech startups and around 50 investors.

The participating startups were selected using criteria including innovation, development stage, market potential, scalability and regulatory readiness.

The initiative is aimed at addressing a common challenge for biotechnology companies: moving from scientific development to commercial production.

Biotechnology businesses can require substantial funding for product development, regulatory processes, manufacturing and market expansion. BIO-NIVESH is designed to connect companies at these stages with investors and potential strategic partners.

The government has described biotechnology as an important part of India's future industrial development and has said the country should not be a late entrant to the next biotechnology-driven industrial revolution.

BIO-NIVESH is intended to continue through subsequent editions in different cities.

The platform has now been launched. Its longer-term impact will depend on the investment and commercialisation that follow.

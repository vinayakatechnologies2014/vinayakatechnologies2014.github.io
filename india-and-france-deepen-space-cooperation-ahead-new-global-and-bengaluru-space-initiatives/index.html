India and France Deepen Space Cooperation Ahead of New Global and Bengaluru Space Initiatives

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Sat, 5 Sep 2026 - 09:18
Verified

India and France are preparing to expand their space partnership, with human spaceflight, advanced technologies and industry cooperation now receiving greater attention.

On September 4, Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh held a virtual meeting with French Minister for Higher Education and Research Philippe Baptiste to discuss upcoming space events and the next phase of India-France cooperation.

France will participate in the upcoming Bengaluru Space Expo, while the French minister is expected to visit ISRO facilities in Bengaluru later this month.

ISRO Chairman S. Somanath has also been invited to deliver a keynote on India's future human spaceflight in Low Earth Orbit at the upcoming International Space Summit in Paris.

The timing is significant because India and France already have a long-running space relationship.

ISRO and France's CNES have worked together on several satellite missions and space programmes. Their cooperation now extends toward areas including human spaceflight, microgravity research and advanced space technologies.

Earlier this year, India and France also agreed to deepen cooperation in microgravity research and human space exploration, giving the partnership a more direct connection to India's human-spaceflight ambitions.

The relationship is therefore moving beyond traditional satellite cooperation.

Human spaceflight creates new requirements in areas such as life-support systems, scientific experiments, astronaut training, microgravity research and technologies needed for sustained activity in space.

Jharkhand: Woman Seeking Police Help Allegedly Raped by Constable Inside Police Station; Five Other Personnel Suspended

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Sun, 1 Nov 2026 - 11:52
Verified

A woman who approached the police seeking protection after a domestic dispute has allegedly been raped by a police constable inside Gamharia police station in Jharkhand's Seraikela-Kharsawan district.

The alleged incident took place on the night of October 29, after the woman reportedly left her home following an argument with her husband. Fearing further violence, she sought police assistance and was taken to the police station in a PCR vehicle.

According to the woman's complaint, police personnel allowed her to rest in a room at the station. However, a wireless operator allegedly entered the room and sexually assaulted her.

The woman reportedly raised an alarm and resisted the assault. Reports say she used a knife kept in the room to defend herself, injuring the constable's finger.

The accused constable was subsequently arrested and sent to judicial custody. Police also suspended five other personnel, including the station officer-in-charge, over alleged negligence, dereliction of duty and failure to respond appropriately to the incident.

An investigation has been initiated, and an Special Investigation Team (SIT) has reportedly been constituted to examine the case. Investigators have also taken steps to preserve evidence, including the seizure of the accused's clothing for forensic examination.

The case raises serious questions about women's safety inside police stations and the accountability of personnel entrusted with protecting the public.

A woman approached the police because she needed protection. The allegation that she was assaulted by a police constable inside the very station where she sought help represents a grave breach of public trust.

The investigation must establish not only what happened inside the room, but also whether other personnel knew about the alleged assault, why they failed to intervene and whether the police station's security arrangements were functioning properly.

Suspending five personnel is an administrative action. The investigation must establish individual responsibility, and the accused must face due process of law.

A police station must be a place where women can seek safety without fear. When the person accused of assault is a police officer, accountability must be transparent and the investigation must be thorough.

Why Is Everyone Talking About Bengaluru's Next Big Technology Event?

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Fri, 31 Jul 2026 - 18:06
Public Buzz

Bengaluru is preparing for the 29th Bengaluru Tech Summit, scheduled for November 17–19, 2026, at the Bangalore International Exhibition Centre. The event will be held under the theme “AI & Beyond”, with artificial intelligence and emerging technologies at the centre of the programme.

The summit is attracting interest because its focus extends well beyond traditional software and IT services. The official programme covers AI, DeepTech, quantum technologies, advanced computing, robotics, aerospace, semiconductors, biotechnology, electric mobility and other emerging technology areas.

Startups are expected to be an important part of the event. BTS 2026 says its startup programme will provide opportunities for entrepreneurs to connect with investors, mentors and industry participants, while the Startup Exhibition Pavilion will showcase new ventures and technologies.

The scale of the exhibition is another reason the event is drawing attention. Organisers say BTS 2026 is expected to feature 1,600+ exhibitors from India and more than 60 countries, along with 1,000+ startups, 450+ corporates, 175+ international exhibitors and 25+ unicorns/venture capital participants. These are organiser projections/targets for the event, rather than attendance figures from a completed summit.

For technology professionals, the event offers a chance to follow discussions around how AI and other emerging technologies are changing businesses and industries. The programme includes conferences, roundtables, CEO-level discussions and technology-focused tracks involving industry, research and policy perspectives.

The summit is also expected to bring greater attention to the relationship between startups, investors, governments and established technology companies. Its programme includes B2B and B2G partnering, an international exhibition, startup and investor activities, and an India-USA Tech Conclave.

For Bengaluru itself, the event carries significance because the city continues to position itself as a major technology and innovation centre. The summit is organised by the Government of Karnataka and Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society, bringing government, industry, startups and research institutions together around the state's technology ecosystem.

What remains to be seen is how much of the conversation around BTS 2026 will translate into new partnerships, investments, startup opportunities and technology collaborations. With the event still several months away, additional speakers, participants, programmes and announcements can continue to emerge.

That is also why the story fits Public Buzz rather than Verified or Developing on its own. The event, date, theme and announced programme are confirmed, while the level of public excitement, expectations and predictions surrounding the summit represent the conversation around the event rather than established facts.

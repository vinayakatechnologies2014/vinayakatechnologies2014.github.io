Is a Major OTT Platform Planning a New Subscription Plan in India?

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Wed, 12 Aug 2026 - 18:07
Rumor

Online discussions about new OTT subscription options in India have raised questions about whether one of the country's major streaming platforms could introduce another plan aimed at Indian viewers. However, no specific platform has officially confirmed the launch of a new India-specific subscription tier based on the information currently available.

The speculation comes at a time when streaming companies are experimenting with different ways to attract and retain subscribers. Mobile-only plans, bundled subscriptions, advertising-supported options and longer-duration packages have become increasingly important in India's price-sensitive entertainment market.

Recent developments show that OTT companies and their partners are already exploring alternative ways to package streaming access. Netflix, for example, entered into a partnership with Flipkart to offer eligible Flipkart Plus members a 30-day Netflix Mobile plan from August 2026, linking streaming access to customer loyalty activity rather than simply selling another standalone subscription tier.

The Indian market is particularly suited to different subscription models because viewers use a wide range of devices and have varying expectations around price and content. TRAI's 2026 consultation material notes the continued growth of connected-TV audiences and the importance of affordability, internet access and the availability of free and paid streaming services in India's OTT market.

The market is also becoming increasingly competitive. India's OTT sector includes major services such as Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, JioHotstar, ZEE5 and SonyLIV, with platforms using different combinations of subscriptions, advertising and bundled access to reach consumers. A government-commissioned industry report notes that bundling with telecom operators and OTT aggregators has become a common customer-acquisition strategy.

That makes the possibility of another India-focused plan commercially interesting, particularly if it offers a lower price, mobile-first access, additional regional content or a combination with other services. However, these are possible features rather than confirmed details of any upcoming plan.

There is also evidence that the wider Indian OTT market continues to grow. Current market projections estimate India's OTT video market could generate around $4.96 billion in revenue in 2026, with video subscription services accounting for a significant portion of that market.

For consumers, the important question would be what a new plan actually offers compared with existing subscriptions. A lower-priced plan could appeal to mobile viewers, while a bundled plan could offer greater value to households already paying for multiple entertainment services.

At present, however, there is not enough verified information to say that a particular major OTT platform is definitely preparing such a plan. Until the company itself announces the product or reliable reporting provides specific evidence, claims about pricing, launch dates, features or eligibility should be treated as speculation.

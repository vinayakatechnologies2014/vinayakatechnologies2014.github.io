Sowjanya Rape-Murder Case: SIT Investigating Officer Lokesh Jagalasar Leads Crime Scene Reconstruction at Dharmasthala

Submitted by Karthik Kumar D K on Sat, 31 Oct 2026 - 18:39
Developing

The Special Investigation Team (SIT) conducting the fresh investigation into the 2012 rape and murder of 17-year-old Sowjanya carried out a crime scene reconstruction at Dharmasthala on October 31.

The exercise was led by SIT investigating officer Lokesh B. Jagalasar, with ACP Manoj Kumar Naik assisting the investigation. The team visited the area near the Nethravathi bathing ghat at Mannasanka, where Sowjanya’s body was found.

Personnel from the Bengaluru Forensic Science Laboratory, a Scene of Crime Officers (SOCO) team, doctors, a village administrative officer and a Public Works Department (PWD) engineer were also present during the exercise. Sowjanya’s uncle, Purandara Gowda, was present as a witness.

The team conducted a spot mahazar and documented the location as part of the fresh investigation. Reports also stated that soil samples were collected during the exercise.

The crime scene reconstruction is part of the reinvestigation ordered by the Supreme Court on September 21, 2026. The court directed the Karnataka government to complete the fresh investigation within three months and submit its report to the competent court.

The SIT had earlier begun examining records and materials from previous investigations. The team’s visit to the location where Sowjanya’s body was found marks another field-level step in the ongoing investigation.

There has been no officially confirmed announcement that the October 31 exercise resulted in the discovery of new evidence or identification of a suspect.

The investigation continues under the reconstituted SIT led by IGP Chetan Singh Rathore.
